🎯 How to use StudyPin for Active Recall

  • Pin Notes Anywhere: Click and drag notes around the corkboard. Pinned notes stay right where you leave them.
  • Link Concepts with Red Yarn: Click "Link Concepts", then click two related notes (like Python with open and CSV DictReader) to visualize connections.
  • Active Recall Mode: Press M to blur code blocks and answers marked with ||answer||. Quiz yourself, then hover or click to verify!
  • Pan & Zoom: Hold Space and drag anywhere on the wall to pan, or use mouse wheel to zoom in and out.
